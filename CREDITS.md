# Credits

- **Colorization and project direction:** [pikamus](https://github.com/pikamus).
- **Original game, artwork and music:** Nintendo and the original creators of _Kid Icarus_ and _Kid Icarus: Of Myths and Monsters_.
- **Emulation and verification:** [PyBoy](https://github.com/Baekalfen/PyBoy).
- **Color-corrected screenshots:** the studio's port of [SameBoy](https://github.com/LIJI32/SameBoy)'s Modern – Accurate conversion, by Lior Halphon. The upstream [Expat license notice](licenses/SameBoy-LICENSE.txt) is included.
- **Hardware reference:** [Pan Docs](https://gbdev.io/pandocs/).

The optional NES themes are adapted for Game Boy audio hardware. This repository distributes the colorization as an IPS patch; original and patched ROMs, game saves, and source music files are not included.

Kid Icarus DX is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.
